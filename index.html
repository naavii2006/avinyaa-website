Company Background

Pioneering Value-Added Agri-Food Processing

Avinyaa Food Processing is an emerging food-processing venture based in Jalna, Maharashtra. Our focus is on developing value-added food and beverage products by combining agricultural resources, modern food processing and consumer-oriented innovation.

We are currently in the pre-launch stage, working on product development, formulation trials, branding, packaging and market preparation. Our initial product range is focused on soybean-based plant beverages.

Meet Our Founders

Building Better Food. Creating Better Opportunities for Farmers.

At Avinyaa Food Processing, we believe that the future of food lies in combining farmer-focused sourcing, food innovation and consumer needs. Our founders bring together experience in agriculture, farmer networks, business development and technology to build a food-processing company that creates value from farm to consumer.

Karan Balchand Bhagure

Founder & CEO

Karan is a young entrepreneur with practical experience across agriculture, agri-business, farmer engagement, renewable energy, and food-processing ventures. Coming from an agriculture-connected background, Karan has worked closely with farmers and understands the challenges they face in getting better value for their produce.

He has served as the CEO of BBF Jalna Farmers Producer Company Limited, gaining hands-on experience in farmer engagement, agricultural commodities, procurement, and trading. He is also the Founder & CEO of Avinyaa Agri Solutions and Avinyaa Renewables Pvt. Ltd.

Vision

"My goal is to build a business that creates value not only after the product reaches the market, but from the farmer level itself—connecting agricultural production, food innovation and consumer needs through a sustainable value chain."

Strategic Alignment

The Challenge & Our Solution

Addressing structural gaps in agricultural commodities through value-added innovation.

The Problem

Industry Challenges

1. Limited Value Addition

A significant opportunity exists to create greater economic value from agricultural produce through processing.

2. Farmer–Market Gap

Agricultural commodities often move through multiple intermediary stages before reaching consumers.

3. Changing Consumer Preferences

Consumers are increasingly exploring alternative food and beverage choices based on health goals.

Our Solution

The Avinyaa Approach

1. Value Addition to Raw Produce

We transform agricultural raw materials into rich, value-added food and beverage products.

2. Soybean Product Focus

Initial focus on developing high-quality soybean-based plant beverages.

3. Scalable Processing Framework

Building a flexible food-processing business model designed to expand into new markets.

Research & Development
