Company Background

Pioneering Value-Added Agri-Food Processing

Avinyaa Food Processing is an emerging food-processing venture based in Jalna, Maharashtra. Our focus is on developing value-added food and beverage products by combining agricultural resources, modern food processing and consumer-oriented innovation.

We are currently in the pre-launch stage, working on product development, formulation trials, branding, packaging and market preparation. Our initial product range is focused on soybean-based plant beverages.

Strategic Alignment

The Challenge & Our Solution

Addressing structural gaps in agricultural commodities through value-added innovation.

The Problem

Industry Challenges

1. Limited Value Addition

A significant opportunity exists to create greater economic value from agricultural produce through processing and tailored product development.

2. Farmer–Market Gap

Agricultural commodities often move through multiple intermediary stages before reaching consumers, missing out on value addition opportunities.

3. Changing Consumer Preferences

Consumers are increasingly exploring alternative food and beverage choices based on taste, convenience, lifestyle and personal health goals.

4. Plant-Based Awareness

Plant-based beverages remain an emerging category. Familiarity, taste, and local availability are critical factors in building broad adoption.

Our Solution

The Avinyaa Approach

1. Value Addition to Raw Produce

We transform agricultural raw materials into rich, value-added food and beverage products right at the regional source.

2. Soybean Product Focus

Initial focus on developing high-quality soybean-based plant beverages and exploring further nutrient applications.

3. Consumer-Centric Flavor Variety

Focus on familiar regional taste profiles (Mango, Ilaychi, Chocolate) to ensure high consumer acceptance.

4. Scalable Processing Framework

Building a flexible, scalable food-processing business model designed to expand into new products and geographical regions.

Value Chain Transformation Comparison

Traditional Commodity Path

  • Farm Gate Raw Soybean Sale
  • Commodity Traders & Middlemen
  • Low Margin Bulk Commodity Export
  • Minimal Farmer Value Realization

Avinyaa Ecosystem Path

  • Farmer-Sourced Quality Crop
  • In-house Value Addition & Processing
  • Premium Consumer-Ready Beverage
  • Shared Prosperity Across Chain
Research & Development

Innovation at Avinyaa

"Innovation for us is not only about creating a new product. It is about finding better ways to convert agricultural resources into products that create value."

Our 5 Core Innovation Pillars

1

Soybean-Based Beverages

Developing smooth, palatable plant-based beverage formulations using soybean as a core raw material, optimizing nutritional retention.

2

Flavour Innovation

Exploring familiar and consumer-friendly Indian and global flavours such as Mango, Chocolate, and Ilaychi (Cardamom) to ease plant-dairy transition.

3

Consumer-Centric Product Development

Utilizing real-time consumer feedback, blind tasting trials, and market response to continually refine mouthfeel and stability.

4

Agricultural Value Addition

Moving away from raw commodity trading towards processing high-value food formats right within the agricultural heartland.

5

Future Product Roadmap

Investigating secondary plant food formats including ready-to-drink options, fortified blends, and functional protein products.
